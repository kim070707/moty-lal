About ChemoCentryx

ChemoCentryx was a biopharmaceutical company founded in 1997. Its research and development activities focused on medicines for inflammatory diseases, autoimmune conditions and cancer.

Amgen completed its acquisition of ChemoCentryx in October 2022. This page is an independent educational overview and is not maintained by either company.
